Как AI меняет маркетинг и рекламу

Короткий ответ:
The number of marketers is decreasing. The number of experiments is increasing. Marketing is atomizing: targeting becomes a purely mathematical thing because there are a shitload of selection tools. The endless brute-forcing of thousands of options by a neural network wins over human intuition. Competition will intensify, and there will be fewer people.

Таргетинг становится математикой

Previously, a targeting specialist would set up 3-5 campaigns and guess which one would work. Today, platforms offer hundreds of options: look-alike, retargeting, interest-based, behavior-based, custom audiences. The choice is so vast that a person physically cannot test all combinations.

The neural network processes thousands of options in minutes. 100 audiences × 50 creatives × 20 bids = 100,000 combinations. A human will check 10. The neural network—all 100,000. And it will select the best 5. This is not a replacement for intuition—it’s mathematics that beats intuition.

Маркетинг атомизируется

Previously, marketing was a "year-long strategy." Now, it's "100 micro-experiments per week." Every channel, every segment, every creative is analyzed separately. Not "the ad performed well on Instagram," but "creative #3 with the speed message showed +18% for the 25-30 age group on Tuesday." Atomic level.

A human cannot think at this level of detail. A neural network can. It sees patterns a human would miss: “on Wednesdays, conversion drops by 7% among men aged 35-40; raise the bid by 5% on Wednesday.”

Less marketers, more results

A team of 10 marketers without AI → 50 campaigns per month. 1 marketer with AI → 200 campaigns. This is not reduction—it’s multiplication. One person does the work of a team. The marketing job market will shrink, but those who remain will earn more—because they manage machines, not Excel spreadsheets.
