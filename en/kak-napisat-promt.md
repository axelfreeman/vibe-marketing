Как написать промпт для нейросети

Короткий ответ:
Forget about "frameworks," "methodologies," and "ideal prompt formulas." Those are gypsy tricks. Start with what you know: just write your question as you think it. A neural network is not a human and not part of culture—it's a library. A modern one. You direct the flow of thoughts into the machine—you get a result.

Why the "perfect prompt" is a myth

The internet is flooded with guides like "10 formulas for the perfect prompt" and "RTF-CTF-ACTOR methodology." The authors of these guides have never generated 200 texts in a day. They wrote one guide — and that's it.

The truth is that neural networks in 2026 understand natural language. They don’t require special incantations. You can write “make it good”—and the model will understand. You can write a half-page stream of consciousness—and the model will figure it out. The quality of the response depends not on a magic formula, but on two things: how clearly you described the task and how good the model itself is.

Where to actually start

Шаг 1:
Write as you think. Don't edit. Don't try to make it "correct." Just dump the task into the chat window.

Шаг 2:
Look at the response. It will most likely be "okay" but not "wow."

Шаг 3:
Clarify. “Make it shorter,” “add numbers,” “write in a different tone,” “you misunderstood, I meant...” — this works better than a new perfect prompt from scratch. The neural network learns through dialogue — use that.

Шаг 4:
After 3-4 iterations, you will have an excellent result. Save this dialogue as a template for similar tasks.

Реальный пример

Плохой промпт:

«Напиши статью про маркетинг»

Хороший промпт:

«Я маркетолог, мне нужна статья для блога на тему "как нейросети меняют поиск работы в 2026". Аудитория: соискатели 25-40 лет. Формат: 3 раздела с подзаголовками, в каждом 2-3 абзаца. Тон: прямой, без воды. Добавь 2-3 конкретных примера с цифрами»

Разница: в первом случае нейросеть гадает. Во втором — у неё есть контекст, аудитория, формат, тон. Результат отличается в 5-10 раз.

Что реально работает
