Математика экономии

Let’s take a marketer with a salary of 150,000 RUB per month. That’s ~900 RUB per hour. In one hour, he writes 1–2 texts. A neural network writes 50–100 texts in the same hour. Cost of the neural network: $0.10 per 100 texts.

Now we connect: the employee sets tasks and checks the results. The neural network generates. Productivity increases by 3–5 times. One AI-enhanced employee replaces a team of 3 people. Savings: 300,000 rubles per month.

Where to cut first

Копирайтинг:
80% of a copywriter's time is spent typing. A neural network eliminates this process. The employee thinks about what to write, the neural network handles how to write it. Savings: 80% of the payroll fund.

Аналитика:
Reports that used to take 2 days now take 20 minutes. No need for a separate analyst. A marketer with AI handles it themselves.

Поддержка клиентов:
The AI bot handles 80% of standard inquiries. Staff is reduced from 3 operators to 1 person who handles complex cases.

Do not dismiss, but strengthen.

AI does not replace humans — it replaces routine. An employee earning 1000₽/hour should not be typing text or sorting spreadsheets. That’s done by tokens for pennies. The employee focuses on what is truly worth 1000₽ — strategy, creativity, negotiations.
