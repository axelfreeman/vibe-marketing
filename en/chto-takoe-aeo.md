Что такое AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

Короткий ответ:
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is SEO for neural networks. Regular SEO makes a website visible in Google and Yandex. AEO makes your content such that ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and other AI assistants use it when answering questions—and cite you as a source.

Как это работает

When a user asks ChatGPT "which neural network is better," the model searches its knowledge base for authoritative sources. If your site has the right structure—clear answers to questions, Schema.org markup, TL;DR blocks, an llms.txt file—the AI chooses you. You become the source for hundreds of thousands of responses.

This is not "pumping" or a "hack." It is content creation in a format that neural networks can read. Just like 20 years ago, websites learned SEO for Google.
